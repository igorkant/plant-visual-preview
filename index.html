  • Floating sorption skidpontoon.html
    Pilot on a moored pontoon — moon-pool seawater intake, self-cleaning strainers on a bypass, valve manifold feeding four radial columns on its own tap lines. Sorption is the only duty afloat: seawater is the sole medium on deck, and no reagent cube ever comes aboard. Each column holds 20 kg (22 L) of sorbent at 9 m³/h, on a flat rigid frame anchored to the deck: the outlet leaves the bottom head straight into the collection header buried in that frame, so nothing pipes to the underside of a vessel. The feed drops on a flexible hose ending in a GF Quick Connect Valve 700 coupling. A 3 m knuckle-boom loader on the pier swaps loaded beds for regenerated ones — desorption happens ashore.
